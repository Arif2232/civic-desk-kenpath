# Service research: uncollected municipal waste in Delhi

Research checked 2 November 2026. Build a **fictional demonstration inspired by** Delhi's Municipal Corporation (MCD) complaint workflow, using invented cases only. It is not MCD 311, connected to the government, or evidence of current service levels.

## What official sources establish

1. **Citizen reports:** MCD's 2025 presentation describes its MCD 311 citizen complaint module for civic issues including garbage, with a photograph and details; complaints can also be made through the web portal or helpline. Its screenshots show category, address, description, location and a generated complaint number, plus a status/feedback view. [Delhi government-hosted MCD presentation](https://environment.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/environment/circulars-orders/1_gda_conf_compressed_0.pdf).
2. **Officer assignment and work:** The same presentation states that citizen complaints are assigned to officers and should be resolved on a priority basis. Its officer screenshots show unassigned/assigned/closed categories, new/reopened/urgent queues, transfer and submit-for-approval actions, and a resolution form with a comment and photos. An adjacent air-pollution programme is described as identification, allocation and resolution; borrowing these three concepts for the demo is a design choice, not proof that every waste case follows identical states. [MCD presentation](https://environment.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/environment/circulars-orders/1_gda_conf_compressed_0.pdf).
3. **Resolution evidence and oversight:** The MCD Commissioner's circular dated **24 August 2023** directed officials to upload a site photograph after redress, particularly for municipal solid waste and construction/demolition waste, and directed zonal deputy commissioners to monitor daily and dispose of complaints within 24 hours. This is historical source material; its present applicability has not been checked, so do not advertise a binding current deadline or guaranteed service time. [MCD circular](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/downloadFile/circular_reg._complaints_lodged_on_mcd_311_app_230824113534834.pdf).
4. **Related grievance handling:** Delhi's government grievance portal describes filing, tracking, reminders and citizen feedback, and says departmental officers can view and act on forwarded complaints. The central CPGRAMS manual separately documents seeking clarification, interim replies, transfer/return to a higher authority and disposal with remarks. These are useful design context, not evidence that MCD 311 has the same exact transition names or permissions. [Delhi CM Jan Sunwai](https://www.cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in/); [NIC CPGRAMS officer manual](https://pgportal.gov.in/CPGOFFICE/Documents/CPGRAMS-Help.pdf).

## Small demo design (proposed, not official policy)

- Focus on **non-removal of household/municipal solid waste**. Seed clearly labeled fictional complaints with a case ID, neighborhood/ward, complaint description, received time, officer assignment and evidence notes; avoid real citizen names, phone numbers, addresses and photographs.
- Model three explicit stages: **received → assigned/in progress → review**. At review, the officer manually chooses **approve resolution**, **reject** or **return for more information/work**, entering a reason. These outcome labels and transition rules are demo simplifications: the sources do not establish them as the exact MCD 311 state machine or legal decision categories. A return should not silently close the complaint.
- Offer an **AI suggestion** at each stage: summarize the report and missing details; suggest a next action or assignment based on the existing report; draft a neutral resolution/return note based on recorded evidence. Show the input and label the text as a draft. Never allow the AI to decide priority, transfer, approve, reject, submit or fabricate a field visit/photo. The officer remains responsible for every consequential action.
- Show an immutable event timeline for receipt, assignment, manual state transitions, decision and explanatory note. Clearly distinguish an AI draft from a human-approved action. A demo history is not evidence of integration with any government system.
- For the pending-work queue, sort deterministically by recorded age and display recorded priority, with an officer able to override; this is a product-design proposal, not an official prioritization formula or guarantee.

## Hindi labels

These are suggested plain-language translations for the demo, **not verified official MCD UI strings**: शिकायत (complaint), शिकायत संख्या (case number), प्राप्त (received), सौंपा गया (assigned), काम जारी (in progress), जाँच के लिए (under review), निस्तारण स्वीकृत करें (approve resolution), अस्वीकार करें (reject), अधिक जानकारी/काम के लिए लौटाएँ (return), कार्रवाई का इतिहास (action history), and एआई सुझाव (AI suggestion). DARPG itself uses terms including शिकायत, लंबित and शिकायत निवारण in its [Hindi CPGRAMS report](https://darpg.gov.in/sites/default/files/CPGRAMS_STATE_UT_Oct2025-hindi.pdf). Confirm the candidate's preferred language before finalizing copy.

## Evaluation ideas (targets to measure, not claimed impact)

In a supervised pilot with real permission and anonymized data, compare median time from receipt to assignment and receipt to verified resolution against a pre-rollout baseline; track the share of cases returned for missing information, and review AI suggestions for unsupported facts. Record the number of eligible cases, period and definitions alongside each measure. The demo has no evidence of measured time savings, government adoption or actual service outcomes.
